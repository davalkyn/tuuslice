GTA 6 is almost here, and this site is now home base for everything we do around it: **news, guides, tips, clips and live streams**, all in one place.

## How it all fits together

- **Telegram** is the news feed. One post per story, straight to your phone, with a link to the full summary here.
- **This site** is the library: every news story labeled verified or rumor, plus guides, tips and the Series S tracker.
- **Discord** is where we hang out: LFG for co-op, the rumor check, clips, and a spoiler channel behind an opt-in role.
- **Kick** is where it all happens live.

## The plan

**Before launch:** three streams a week. News reactions, a live Series S setup test and community nights. Clips go out daily on YouTube and TikTok.

**Launch week:** scheduled launch streams with spoiler rules on, and **Series S performance coverage within the first 48 hours**.

**After launch:** guides for missions, money and settings, updated as we play.

## How you can help

Follow on [Kick](https://kick.com/tuuslice) so you get the go-live ping, join the [Discord](/community/), and share posts you find useful. It all helps a small channel get found. If you want to go further, there's a [support page](/support/) too.
