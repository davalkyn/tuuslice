The 60 FPS debate is over for launch. In a Newswire post on **November 10**, Rockstar confirmed that **GTA 6 targets 30 FPS on every console**: PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S.

## What Rockstar confirmed

- **30 FPS target on all consoles.** Rockstar says the target was chosen to keep the density and visual detail of Leonida's open world.
- **PS5 Pro gets better image quality, not more frames:** PSSR upscaling and ray-traced shadows. No 60 FPS mode has been announced.
- **Xbox:** Quick Resume is supported, and the game launches on Xbox Cloud Gaming, where the cloud version uses the Series S settings.

## What's still unknown

- **Series S resolution.** Rockstar hasn't published it. The earlier 720p figure was [analysis, not confirmation](/news/gta-6-series-s-performance-rumors/).
- **How steady 30 is.** A "target" isn't a guaranteed lock in busy scenes.
- **Extra graphics modes.** None announced. Reports note Rockstar hasn't explicitly ruled them out for later.

## Our take

If you were waiting on a 60 FPS console mode before buying, it isn't coming at launch. We'll test how well the Series S holds 30 live on [Kick](https://kick.com/2slice) from launch night and log it in the [Series S tracker](/series-s/).
