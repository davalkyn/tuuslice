Screenshots are going around showing GTA 6 with a **perfect 5/5 on the PlayStation Store** before anyone can play it. Here's what's actually going on.

## What was found

A site that tracks the PlayStation Store's backend spotted **11 user ratings** on the GTA 6 listing, averaging 5/5.

## Why it doesn't mean much

- **Eleven ratings is a tiny sample.**
- They most likely come from **media and testers with early access**, not regular players.
- They're star ratings only. There are no written reviews and no critic scores.

## Verdict

**Not a review score.** Real reviews are [reported for November 18](/news/gta-6-review-embargo-november-18/). Until then, ignore any "GTA 6 gets a perfect score" headlines.
