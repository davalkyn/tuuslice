GTA 6 is one of the biggest console installs ever. Rockstar's official figure is **up to 212 GB of available storage**, depending on your device.

## The numbers being reported

- **Official:** up to 212 GB of free storage needed.
- **PS5 preload screens:** about 209.75 GB to download, about 212 GB installed.
- **Xbox store listing:** about 225 GB. Xbox counts storage units differently, so this looks like the same data shown another way.
- **One tracker** lists the PS5 build at about 223 GB.

The exact number depends on where you read it, so the safe move is to **keep 225 GB or more free**.

## What that means for your console

- **Xbox Series S (512 GB):** about 364 GB is usable, so GTA 6 takes well over half of it. The 1 TB model or an expansion card makes life much easier.
- **PS5:** the launch model has about 667 GB usable. GTA 6 takes roughly a third.
- **Remember:** Series X|S games must run from the internal drive or an official expansion card. A USB drive can only store them.
