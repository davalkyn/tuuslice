You can download GTA 6 right now. Rockstar moved the digital preload **forward to November 10** (it was originally November 12), so there's more than a week to get it installed before launch.

## Preload timing

- **PS5 (digital):** opened at midnight local time on November 10.
- **Xbox Series X|S (digital):** opened worldwide at 12:00 AM GMT on November 10.
- **Disc copies:** the codes in boxed copies are reported to become redeemable from **November 12**.
- **Game unlocks:** midnight local time on [November 19](/news/gta-6-release-time-midnight-local/).

## How to start it

- **PS5:** open your Game Library, find Grand Theft Auto VI, and choose Download. Check that auto-downloads are on under Settings → Saved Data and Game/App Settings.
- **Xbox:** go to My games & apps → Full library → Owned games and install it. You can also queue it from the Xbox mobile app.

## Before you press download

It's a huge install: [up to 212 GB](/news/gta-6-file-size-212-gb/). On a Series S that's most of the internal drive, so [clear space first](/tips/series-s-storage-before-launch/). On a slow connection, start today rather than on the 18th.
