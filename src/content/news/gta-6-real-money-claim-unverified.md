You may have seen headlines saying GTA 6 will let players **earn real money**. Here's where that comes from, and why we're filing it under **unverified**.

## The claim

Italian outlet Money.it repeated a claim from the creator **HipHopGamer** that GTA 6's user-generated content tools, including an area for recording videos and making short films, could let players earn real money.

## What's actually known

- **No official detail.** Rockstar hasn't announced any real-money earning system for GTA 6.
- **Mods are tightly controlled.** Rockstar's [mod guidelines](/news/rockstar-mod-guidelines-creator-platform/) only allow monetization through its official Creator Platform, and they ban selling in-game currency for real money and anything crypto.
- **Hiring isn't a launch.** Rockstar is hiring for creator monetization tools, but that's a job listing, not a feature announcement.

## Verdict

**Unverified.** Don't build plans, or spend money, on it. And watch out for "early access" or "earn crypto in GTA 6" sites: they're scams.
