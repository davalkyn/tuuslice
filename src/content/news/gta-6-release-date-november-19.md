**Grand Theft Auto VI launches on Thursday, November 19, 2026** for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick reaffirmed that date on an earnings call, after the game moved from its original fall 2025 window.

## What's confirmed

- **Date:** November 19, 2026
- **Platforms:** PS5, Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S
- **PC:** not announced. Most coverage expects a PC version later, most likely in 2027, but that's an estimate, not a Rockstar statement.

## What it means for you

- If you're on **Series S**, check your free storage now. Rockstar hasn't published an install size yet, and the Series S has the smallest drive of the three consoles. Our [Series S tracker](/series-s/) will fill in the real number on launch day.
- If you're waiting on **PC**, there's no official date to plan around yet. Don't trust "leaked" PC dates until Rockstar posts one.
