Rockstar's support page, updated November 3, says GTA 6 releases at **midnight local time on November 19, 2026, in all regions**, on both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## What that means

- **South Africa:** 00:00 SAST on Thursday, November 19.
- **UK:** 00:00 GMT on November 19.
- **Everywhere else:** midnight on your own clock, so players in New Zealand and Australia get in first and the launch rolls west from there.

## One open question

Coverage notes a wrinkle for countries with several time zones, like the US and Canada: Rockstar's wording points to midnight in each time zone, while PlayStation describes the rollout by country. If you're there, check your console's store page for the exact unlock time.
