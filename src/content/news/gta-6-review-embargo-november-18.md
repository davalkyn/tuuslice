Wondering when you'll see scores? Reports say the **GTA 6 review embargo lifts on November 18**, one day before launch.

## Where this comes from

- The date was first posted by the leaker **NateTheHate**.
- **Insider Gaming** says it independently verified November 18.
- One report puts the time at **6 AM Eastern** (1 PM in South Africa). That detail is the least certain.

## What's confirmed

Nothing official. **Rockstar hasn't announced an embargo date or time**, so this stays a rumor until it does.

## If it's right

Reviews would arrive roughly a day before the midnight unlock, enough time to read a few before you play. We'll round up the first scores here and on [Telegram](https://t.me/+6RiYfefXQ8Y1MjI0) when they land, spoiler-free.
