If you're playing GTA 6 on an **Xbox Series S**, set your expectations now. Early analysis points to a big gap between the Series S and the other two consoles, though **none of it is confirmed by Rockstar.**

## What's being reported

- **Resolution:** GTA BOOM reports that Digital Foundry expects about **720p on Series S**, against roughly **1440p at 30 fps** on PS5 and Series X. The gap is tied to the Series S's smaller memory.
- **Frame rate:** a rumor reported by Digital Citizen says the Series S is targeting **30 fps**, and that a 60 fps mode for PS5 and Series X may come at launch or later.
- **Other cutbacks:** the same analysis expects lower-quality texture streaming, volumetrics and shadows on Series S.

These predictions come from trailer footage and hardware specs, **not gameplay**.

## What Rockstar has said

Nothing specific. Rockstar hasn't published performance targets, graphics modes or PS5 Pro enhancements. Take-Two's CEO has said its labels are good at supporting platforms with different levels of tech, but that isn't a performance promise.

## Our take

Plan around **30 fps and a reduced resolution** on Series S, and don't buy on the promise of smooth performance until it's been tested. We'll test it live on launch day and update the [Series S tracker](/series-s/) within 48 hours.
