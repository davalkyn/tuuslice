Rockstar published updated **mod guidelines on September 10** that apply across its catalogue, **GTA 6 included**. The headline rule for anyone hoping to earn from mods: **monetization goes through Rockstar's official Creator Platform, and every other mod has to be non-commercial.**

## The key rules

- **Money:** mods can only be monetized through the official Creator Platform. Everything else is non-commercial.
- **No real-money mechanics:** no chance-based mechanics involving real money, no selling in-game currency for cash, and no crypto.
- **Story is off-limits:** mods can't extend or rewrite Rockstar's storylines, missions or characters.
- **No mixing games:** maps, characters and assets can't be moved from one Rockstar game into another.
- **Multiplayer and ports:** multiplayer modding needs the Creator Platform or a direct license, and unofficial ports are out.

## Why it matters

Digiday reported that Rockstar has been hiring for its creator platforms team, including a role to build **creator monetization tools**. That points to a bigger creator economy around GTA 6, but Rockstar hasn't announced a GTA 6 creator program yet, and there's no date for one.

For streamers, note that these are **mod** rules. Rockstar hasn't published GTA 6 rules for streaming or video monetization. We'll post them here and on Telegram the moment it does.
