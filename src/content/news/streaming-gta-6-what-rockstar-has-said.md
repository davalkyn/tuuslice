If you stream or make videos, the question is simple: **what are the rules for GTA 6 content?** Right now, **Rockstar hasn't published any.**

## What happened with the Netflix reveal

In August, creators weren't sure whether they could livestream Rockstar's extended look at GTA 6 on Netflix, or only react to it. Rockstar posted a clarification a few hours later: creators could stream it, **as long as they followed each platform's rules and added commentary or context** rather than rebroadcasting the raw video.

## What we know about the future

- Digiday reported that Rockstar is hiring for its creator platforms team, including building **creator monetization tools**. Things may change before or after launch.
- There are **no published GTA 6 streamer rules** yet: not for monetization, not for spoilers, not for footage.

## What we're doing

- Always streaming with **commentary**, never raw footage.
- Checking Rockstar's official channels before streaming any new Rockstar footage.
- Posting the rules here and on [Telegram](https://t.me/+6RiYfefXQ8Y1MjI0) as soon as they're published.
