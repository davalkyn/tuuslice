Launch week is going to be loud. If you can't play on day one, here's how to keep the story fresh.

## Mute the obvious

- On **X**, go to Settings → Privacy and safety → Mute and block → **Muted words**. Add *GTA 6 ending*, *GTA 6 spoilers*, *Lucia*, *Jason* and *Leonida ending* for 14 days.
- On **YouTube**, use "Don't recommend channel" on any channel that posts thumbnails with story moments.
- On **TikTok**, long-press spoiler videos → **Not interested**.

## Turn off autoplay

Autoplaying previews are where most spoilers happen. Turn off autoplay on YouTube's home feed and on Discord's GIF and embed previews (User Settings → Chat, called Text & Images in older versions).

## Use the opt-in spoiler channel

In our [Discord](/community/), story talk lives in **#spoilers**, which is hidden until you pick the role. Everywhere else is spoiler-free for the first two weeks.

## On stream

Our launch streams run with **spoiler rules on**: no story talk in chat ahead of where the stream is. Mods will time out anyone who tests it.
